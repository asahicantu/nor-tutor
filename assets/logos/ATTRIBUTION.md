# Logos

Site icons shown next to each entry on the Sources page. They are trademarks of their respective owners and are
used only to identify the site they belong to. Each file was downloaded once from that site on 2026-10-11 by
`node tools/fetch-logos.mjs` (icons over 64 KB, or anything that is not a real image, are skipped; the Sources page
then shows the entry's emoji instead). Re-run the tool after adding sources; files are named after the entry id.
